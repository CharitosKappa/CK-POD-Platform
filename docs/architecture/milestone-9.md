# Milestone 9 — Analytics, Lifecycle & CX Operations

Analytics events are platform-owned and idempotent. Canonical dimensions are `sessionId`, `userId`, `orderId`, style family, preset and preset version; events may have no project context (for example a visitor session). Financial KPIs use order/payment snapshots, never browser events; tax collected is excluded from revenue. The dashboard provides Visitor → Generation, generation success, Generation → Purchase, Add-to-Cart → Purchase, generations/order, AOV, refund/reprint/defect and production-rejection rates. CAC and LTV are `UNAVAILABLE`; gross and contribution margin are `INCOMPLETE` until acquisition-spend and direct-cost inputs are present. It never substitutes zero for unknown data.

Refunds reserve a unique pending idempotency record under an order lock before the provider request. The cumulative successful-or-pending amount cannot exceed captured payment, provider refund IDs are persisted, and every request/outcome is recorded in the immutable operational audit history. Reprints are separate lineage records to the original order, item and fulfillment, with structured reason and cost. Trusted approval revalidates provider qualification (enabled provider, qualified/active, technical/G3/physical-test/shipping checks) and never submits fulfillment directly; a future replacement must still traverse M7 routing, compliance, readiness and explicit submission. Provider defects preserve order/item/product/variant/provider/fulfillment/reprint attribution and a controlled taxonomy; small samples do not alter routing scores automatically.

Lifecycle delivery is provider-neutral. The fake adapter is local/CI default and Klaviyo is optional. Transactional order/shipping/delivery communications are distinct from marketing welcome, saved-project, generated-no-purchase, cart/checkout abandonment, review and reorder intents. All delivery keys are idempotent; purchase suppresses pending abandonment. The worker-facing abandonment/reorder processors take explicit delay configuration, making timing environment-configurable. Payloads omit prompts, assets, policy evidence and provider economics. Ops visibility is a single trusted surface for refunds, reprints, defects, credits, generation and async failures, lifecycle delivery failures, customer notes, and audit history.
