# Milestone 8 — Moderation & Compliance Policy Engine

## Policy flow

```text
Prompt / exact text ──> PROMPT_PRE_GENERATION ──BLOCK──> no provider call / no credit
Reference upload ────> REFERENCE_UPLOAD ───────REVIEW──> internal rejection / trusted review
Generated bytes ─────> GENERATED_OUTPUT ───────BLOCK──> no delivery / no credit
Production master ───> FINAL_ARTWORK_PRE_PRODUCTION ──> M7 compliance review ─> readiness
```

The platform records `ALLOW`, `BLOCK`, `REVIEW`, and `UNKNOWN`, rather than a boolean. Classifiers return
structured categories such as fan art/characters, brand-logo risk, public-person likeness, protected text,
adult content, violence/weapons, and uncertainty. The current deterministic local classifier deliberately
uses explicit fixtures; it is not legal or visual-IP clearance and future real classifiers are adapter
implementations behind the same policy contract.

## Human review and production safety

Machine evaluations and human decisions are separate immutable records. Trusted Ops roles can make an
audited compliance decision; consumers never receive internal findings or reviewer APIs. `BLOCK` remains
ineligible. `REVIEW` and `UNKNOWN` require a human approval. The M7 readiness snapshot requires a final
evaluation for the exact ordered project version/production master, so an older result cannot authorize a
changed final design.

Fan art is prohibited in the MVP. Political expression without a recognizable-person likeness is not
blanket-blocked. Brand, logo, and protected-text signals are intentionally reviewed when context needs
human/legal judgment. Consumer errors stay concise and do not reveal thresholds or classifier internals.

## G5

M8 supplies technical records, review controls, and fail-safe gates for external legal review. It does not
complete G5, make ownership determinations, or provide copyright/trademark/legal approval.
